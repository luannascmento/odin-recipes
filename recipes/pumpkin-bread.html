Pumpkin Bread

Description

A traditional, spicy and great for holidays is a great old Maine recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 (15-ounce) can pumpkin puree
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • ⅔ cup water
  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ½ teaspoon ground cloves
  • ¼ teaspoon ground ginger

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour two 9x5-inch loaf pans.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and ginger together in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk pumpkin purée, eggs, oil, water, and sugar together in a separate large bowl until smooth and well combined.
  4. Stir the flour mixture into the pumpkin mixture just until no dry streaks remain; do not overmix.
  5. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared loaf pans.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of each loaf comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, about 50 minutes
  7. Cool the loaves in their pans for 10 minutes. Run a thin knife around the edges, then turn the loaves onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing or wrapping.
